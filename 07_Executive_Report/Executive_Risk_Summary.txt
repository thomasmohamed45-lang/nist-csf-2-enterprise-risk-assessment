1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
==================================================

This simulated assessment evaluates ten cybersecurity
risk scenarios affecting critical business systems
at Pine Ridge Logistics, Inc.

The assessment uses NIST CSF 2.0 as its organizational
framework and a project-defined likelihood-impact
scoring methodology informed by NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1.

All ratings and existing controls are hypothetical.
No production systems were accessed or tested.

3. TOP PRIORITY CYBERSECURITY RISKS
==================================================

RSK-008 - Backup data is deleted or encrypted during a ransomware incident
Residual risk: 20/25 (Critical)
Accountable owner: IT Manager
Recommended action: Implement immutable or offline backups; isolate backup administration; perform full restoration exercises.
Proposed timeline: Proposed: 0-30 days after approval

RSK-001 - Phishing compromises an employee Microsoft 365 account
Residual risk: 16/25 (High)
Accountable owner: IT Security Lead
Recommended action: Enforce phishing-resistant MFA for high-risk accounts; configure Conditional Access; monitor suspicious sign-ins.
Proposed timeline: Proposed: 0-60 days after approval

RSK-006 - Transportation management system becomes unavailable
Residual risk: 16/25 (High)
Accountable owner: Logistics Director
Recommended action: Establish tested TMS recovery procedures; evaluate failover capability; define approved recovery objectives.
Proposed timeline: Proposed: 0-60 days after approval

RSK-007 - Customer information database is accessed without authorization
Residual risk: 15/25 (High)
Accountable owner: IT Security Lead
Recommended action: Strengthen database least privilege; monitor sensitive queries; review access and data protection controls.
Proposed timeline: Proposed: 0-60 days after approval

RSK-004 - Ransomware compromises Windows domain controllers
Residual risk: 15/25 (High)
Accountable owner: IT Manager
Recommended action: Harden domain controllers; restrict administrative access; improve endpoint detection and network segmentation.
Proposed timeline: Proposed: 0-60 days after approval

5. MANAGEMENT RECOMMENDATIONS
==================================================

1. Prioritize ransomware-resistant backup protection
   and documented restoration testing.

2. Validate high-risk identity and access controls,
   including privileged access and MFA.

3. Strengthen monitoring and operational resilience
   for enterprise cloud and logistics systems.

4. Review third-party security assurance and
   incident notification requirements.

5. Collect evidence before confirming control
   effectiveness or updating residual risk scores.
